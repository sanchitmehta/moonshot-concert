Moonshot Fest

Architecture

Two teams and two clouds. The Storefront team runs the website and its API in Azure. The Data Platform team runs the system of record: its API, catalog and cache in Azure, its core database and billing in AWS.

No hop uses a key or a password. The storefront-api never connects to a database or a cache, and only billing reads the billing schema.

A reservation, end to end

  1. The guest picks a pass and joins its line. The browser asks for its place every 5 seconds.
  2. The admission worker admits the next guests from each queue every 10 seconds. An admitted guest has 10 minutes to reserve.
  3. The data-platform-api checks the sale window, price, quantity and stock in one PostgreSQL transaction and holds the passes.
  4. It asks billing to charge the guest's Moonshot Wallet. Billing writes the debit, the payment and the invoice in one transaction.
  5. Paid: the reservation is confirmed and the guest gets a private confirmation link. Refused: the hold is cancelled and the passes return to stock.
